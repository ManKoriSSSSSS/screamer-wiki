FNAF.mov

EXPIRED DOMAIN BROKEN SCRIPT

FNAF.mov was a short-lived bait website that tried to capitalize on the Five Nights at Freddy's hype. It’s one of those sites that relied entirely on a single click to do its thing, but eventually, it just became a dead link that redirects to nowhere.

The Screamer

If you visited the site back when it was actually "functional," you'd be greeted by a blank page or a fake loading screen. Clicking anywhere on the screen triggered a full-screen image of Pazuzu (the demon from The Exorcist) accompanied by a high-pitched, distorted scream.

The weirdest part? After the screamer finished its jump, the site would automatically redirect you to uncyclopedia.co. Nobody really knows why the creator chose that specific site. Maybe they thought it was a "funny" prank, or maybe they just liked satirical wikis. Either way, it made zero sense. RANDOM AS HELL

Why it's "Broken"

Even if you find a mirror of this site today, it’s probably not going to work. Modern browsers (Chrome, Firefox, etc.) have basically nuked autoplaying audio. Since the site's script relied on immediate sound, it usually just sits there in silence or requires a manual click that most people aren't going to give a dead site.

"The domain is expired anyway. If you click it now, you just get a 'This site can’t be reached' message or some squatter's ad page. Boring." — Admin_Josh
